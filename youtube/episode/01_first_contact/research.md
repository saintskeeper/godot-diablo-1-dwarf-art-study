# Episode 01: First Contact - Research Summary

## Video Overview

- **Title Working:** Week 1: First Contact
- **Target Length:** ~10 minutes
- **Style:** Come along on my journey / learning log format
- **Theme:** "Touch every tool, fear nothing"

## Target Audience

### Primary
- Aspiring game developers who feel overwhelmed by the tool stack
- Beginners paralyzed by "where do I start?"
- People who want to learn game dev but have limited time (jobs, families)

### Secondary
- Solo devs interested in the full-stack approach (art, code, music, design)
- AI-curious creators who want a balanced perspective
- Content creators documenting their own learning journeys

## Content Pillars

### 1. Tools Touched (5 in one week)
- Procreate (iPad drawing)
- Blender (3D modeling + viewport navigation)
- Godot (game engine + scripting)
- OP-Z (music production)
- AI assistants (curriculum planning, prototyping)

### 2. Tangible Output
- "Unstable Table" - grid-based tactical PvP game
- Viking theme with Event Die mechanic
- GBA-style shader aesthetic
- Shipped ugly, but shipped

### 3. Controversial Hook
- AI in creativity: accessibility angle vs. replacement fears
- "AI makes creativity available to people without time"
- Personal stance: augments learning, doesn't replace doing

## Competitive Landscape

### Similar Content
- Game dev journey videos (Blackthornprod, Brackeys legacy, Heart Beast)
- "I made a game in X days" challenge format
- Tool tutorial channels (individual tools)

### Content Gap Identified
- Most journey videos focus on ONE tool or ONE game
- Few show the full-stack approach (art + code + music + design)
- Weekly episodic format is underutilized
- AI perspective often polarized - nuanced take is rare

## Strategic Angles

### High-Potential Hooks
1. **Quantity of tools** - "5 tools in 1 week" creates curiosity
2. **Fear removal** - "The tools don't bite" resonates with paralyzed beginners
3. **Shipped ugly** - Normalizes imperfection, reduces viewer anxiety
4. **AI controversy** - Engagement driver, shareability

### Unique Value Proposition
- Not a tutorial channel - a learning journal
- Viewer grows WITH the creator
- Full-stack visibility (rare in gamedev content)
- Weekly accountability structure

## Thumbnail/Title Insights

### What Works in This Niche
- Before/after comparisons
- Tool logos arranged together
- "Week X" serialized branding
- Faces showing genuine emotion (overwhelm, surprise, accomplishment)
- Game footage as proof of output

### What to Avoid
- Generic "I made a game" without specificity
- Tutorial energy when it's a journey format
- Overcrowded thumbnails
- Misleading complexity levels

## Key Metrics to Watch

- Watch time through Act 2 (the shipped game reveal)
- Comments about viewer's own tool fears
- Shares on AI section (controversial = shareable)
- Week 2 subscriber retention
