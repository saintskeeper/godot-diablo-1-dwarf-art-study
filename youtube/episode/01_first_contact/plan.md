### Hook Script (0:00 - 0:15)

**Pattern:** Problem Amplification

```
[0:00 - 0:03] Screen recording: cursor hovering over Blender icon
AUDIO: "I stared at this icon for three months."

[0:03 - 0:05] Cursor still hovering, slight movement but no click
AUDIO: "Never clicked it."

[0:05 - 0:09] Desktop with all 5 tool icons visible
AUDIO: "Blender. Godot. Procreate. A synth I bought two years ago. And... AI."

[0:09 - 0:12] Cut to: Unstable Table gameplay with GBA shader
AUDIO: "By Sunday, I'd touched all five. And shipped this."

[0:12 - 0:15] Hold on game, then transition
AUDIO: "This is Week 1."

[0:15+] → Begin Act 1 (The Tools)
```

**Why this hook works:**
- Validates viewer's own paralysis ("I do that too")
- Escalates stakes: 3 months avoidance → 1 week action
- Shows proof (shipped game) within 12 seconds
- Sets up episodic "Week X" structure
