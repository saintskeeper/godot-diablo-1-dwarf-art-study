## Tips for All Prompts

- Add `--no shadows` for completely flat assets
- Use `--s 50` for less stylization
- Use `--q 2` for higher detail
- Adjust `--ar` ratio as needed for taller/wider objects
- Add `--seed [number]` to maintain consistency across asset set
