# WaltMakes Banner Spec: "Cozy Forge Workshop"

**Dimensions:** 1200 x 400px (16:5 ratio)

---

## Background Layer

- Weathered **Parchment** (`#F5EDE0`) texture
- Subtle paper grain/noise — not pristine, has character
- Faint broken **ensō circle** in **Fog** (`#A8A498`) at ~15% opacity, positioned right-of-center

---

## Focal Element: Duck Viking

- Positioned left third of banner
- Sitting on a warm **Hearth Orange** (`#E07040`) circle/blob shape
- **Sketchy white outline** around the mascot (2-4px, hand-drawn wobble)
- Holding a pixel art hammer or brush — "builder at work" energy
- Drop shadow: soft, warm, not harsh

---

## Typography (Right Side)

| Element | Font | Color |
|---------|------|-------|
| "WaltMakes" | **Futura Medium** | Deep Ink `#2A2520` |
| `// cozy forge` | **SF Mono** | Viking Teal `#3B8D9A` |

---

## Accent Details

- Small teal paint splatter or pixel dust near the ensō
- Optional: tiny orange sparks near the hammer
