## Weekly Study Structure

```
MONDAY    — Pick the scene, post reference screenshot
TUESDAY   — Break down objects, post asset list
WED/THU   — Generate & iterate assets
FRIDAY    — Assemble scene, post comparison
WEEKEND   — Workflow notes, prep next week
```

### Study Log Format
Each week gets a folder: `notes/week-{n}/`
- `{day}-art.md` — Reference images, inspirations
- `objects-in-scene/` — Asset breakdowns
- `asset-prompts/` — Generation prompts that worked
- `outputs/` — Final renders, iterations
- `retro.md` — What worked, what didn't, what to carry forward

# Market Research

Based on current industry analysis, here's the 2025 meta for indie game success:

## The "Fast-Follow Micro Genre" Strategy

The biggest shift right now is toward **rapid development and trend-riding**. According to industry analysts, the safest approach for most developers is to try out a new fast-follow micro genre with the goal of releasing within 4-6 months. Games like Desktop Defender took only about 2 weeks to develop and became one of the most played demos at Steam Next Fest October 2025.

## Hot Genres & Mechanics

**"Friend-slop" co-op horror** remains dominant. Phasmophobia and Lethal Company set the standard, and horror co-op games are hitting success simultaneously and more frequently.

Other winning formulas include:
- **"Crafty, Buildy, Simulation, Management" games** — think Factorio, Rimworld, Manor Lords
- Hybrid genres like narrative + survival, co-op + roguelike are rising, with titles like Schedule 1 (drug-dealing sim) and Knights in Tight Spaces (deck-building roguelike) exemplifying this trend.
- Narrative-driven games doubled horror games in achieving 1,000+ reviews in early 2025.

## Polish Is Overrated (Right Now)

Indie games don't need to be overworked and overpolished—when you pick a game in a trending genre, steam shoppers are so hungry they'll consume anything that resembles the new trend before it's even fully cooked.

## Marketing Essentials

- Short-form content on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts have become primary drivers for indie virality—the challenge is creating hooks that align with meme culture.
- Build community early on Discord, Reddit, and itch.io devlogs
- Avoid launch discounts—they don't significantly increase conversions and reduce your game's perceived value.
- Avoid launching in crowded seasons like fall (AAA-heavy). Consider less saturated windows like August or spring.

## The Portfolio Approach

One successful developer strategy: "I'm doing 6 games in 12 months—1 bigger one for prestige/deals, and smaller ones to fill in revenue gaps."

The core message: this is considered a golden window for scrappy, fast-moving indie devs who can ride emerging micro-trends rather than spending years polishing a passion project.
