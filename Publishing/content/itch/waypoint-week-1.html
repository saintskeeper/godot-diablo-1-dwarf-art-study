The Study

This was pure nostalgia fuel. I wanted to capture that feeling of stepping into Tristram for the first time—the snow falling, the stone waypoint glowing, that sense of lonely adventure.

Built in Godot 4.6 as a 30-second proof of concept. The real focus was nailing the isometric perspective and recreating those iconic UI orbs.

Free Assets Included

The Dwarven UI kit is included—use it in your own projects:

  • Health Orb — Red orb with animated fill shader
  • Mana Orb — Blue orb with matching shader
  • Fill Shaders — Smooth drain/fill with customizable empty color
  • GDScript Components — Ready-to-use health/mana bar scripts
Inspiration

Old Tristram. Act 5 waypoints. That Blizzard North magic. The UI draws heavily from Diablo's dwarven metalwork aesthetic—ornate frames, glowing orbs, chunky potion belts.
