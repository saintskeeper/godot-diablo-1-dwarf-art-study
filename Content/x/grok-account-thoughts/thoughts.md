Your profile is your billboard—make it irresistible so profile visits convert to follows.
- **Handle**: @WaltMakes is solid—short, memorable, implies creation/making (what niche? Building, art, tech, content? Clarify ASAP).
- **Bio**: Right now it's blank or default—huge missed opportunity. Make it punchy: "Building [your thing] one project at a time | Sharing wins, fails, and how-tos | Indie maker/hacker/artist?" Add emojis, a call-to-action like "DM for collabs" or a link (e.g., Linktree to your site/YouTube/GitHub).
- **PFP**: Update to something clear and branded (your face, logo, or creation). Avoid defaults.
- **Banner**: Eye-catching image related to your content (e.g., your workspace, a project).
- **Pin**: Once you have your first strong post/thread, pin it.
- **Bonus**: Get X Premium if possible—verified accounts get reply boosts and better distribution.

This setup can turn 20-30% of profile visitors into followers.

#### Top "Hacks" Working Right Now for New Accounts
- **Reply Strategy (Your Superpower)**: Target mid-size accounts (5K-50K followers) in your niche. Add value—insights, humor, questions—not "Great post!". Early replies (first 10-20) get top placement.
- **First 15 Minutes Rule**: When you post, self-engage if needed (but focus on real interactions). High early velocity = algo push.
- **Media Every Time**: No plain text—add pics/videos/GIFs.
- **Build Mutuals**: Engage small accounts loyally—they reciprocate and become your early community.
- **Avoid Pitfalls**: No spam, copy-paste, or low-effort (e.g., emoji-only replies). Stay authentic—share doubts/fails alongside wins.
- **Timing**: Post when your audience is active (check analytics later; start with 8-11 AM or evenings EST).
