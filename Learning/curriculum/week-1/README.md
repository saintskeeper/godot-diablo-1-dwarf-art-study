# Week 1 Resources

> **Theme:** First Contact — Touch every tool, fear nothing

## This Week's Focus

Week 1 is about removing fear. You'll open every tool in your stack, do something small in each, and prove to yourself that none of them will bite. By Sunday, you'll have an ugly L-shaped tower that shoots at cubes with music playing. It won't be pretty. It will be shipped.

**Study Game:** Kingdom Rush (2+ hrs across the week)
**POC Goal:** A cube that shoots a sphere at another cube in Godot

## If You Get Stuck

1. **Re-read the Quick Reference** section in each day's resource file
2. **Search YouTube** for "[tool] [specific problem] tutorial"
3. **Check official docs** linked above
4. **Ask in Discord/Reddit** — Godot, Blender, OP-Z communities are helpful
5. **Move on** — If something takes >30 min, note it and continue. Don't let one blocker stop the whole week.
