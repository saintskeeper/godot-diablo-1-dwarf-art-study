# WaltMakes Brand Identity

## Core Concept: "Cozy Forge"

A fusion of **cassette futurism** (analog tech warmth) and **wabi-sabi** (imperfect beauty), refined through the **Duck Viking** mascot's vivid teal-orange palette.

**The vibe:** Scrappy builder. Learning in public. The workshop floor, not the showroom.

## Color Philosophy

- **Complementary harmony** — Teal + Orange (~170° apart) creates energy without harshness
- **No pure white/black** — Always warm. Deep Ink has character, Parchment has soul.
- **Saturation matched** — Extended colors (green, red) tuned to sit with accents, not compete
- **Derived from mascot** — Colors pulled from Duck Viking for cohesion

---

## Typography

### Primary: Inter
Clean, technical, readable. Modern sans-serif.
- **Bold (700)** — Brand name, display
- **Semibold (600)** — Headings
- **Regular (400)** — Body text

### Monospace: Space Mono
Terminal feel for code, timestamps, technical content.

### CSS
```css
--wm-font-sans: 'Inter', 'IBM Plex Sans', -apple-system, sans-serif;
--wm-font-mono: 'Space Mono', 'JetBrains Mono', monospace;
```

## Logo & Mascot

### Primary: Duck Viking
A cartoon duck viking warrior — cozy, approachable, memorable.
- Embodies the "scrappy builder" energy
- Colors match brand palette exactly
- Works at all sizes

### Secondary: Maker's Circle
A broken ensō (Zen brush circle) — work in progress, always learning.
- Use when mascot doesn't fit
- Available in Sumi Ink or Viking Teal

## Voice & Tone

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| Curious learner | "I'm figuring this out" energy |
| Scrappy builder | Show the messy middle |
| Authentic | Credit inspirations, ask questions publicly |
| Work in progress | Never claim expertise you don't have |

---

## Usage Guidelines

### Do
- Use generous whitespace
- Let the teal-orange pair do the heavy lifting
- Embrace warmth in everything
- Show process, not just results

### Don't
- Use harsh neon colors
- Over-polish to sterility
- Use pure black or white backgrounds
- Hide the learning journey
